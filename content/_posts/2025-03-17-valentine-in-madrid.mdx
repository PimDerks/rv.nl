On Saturday, January 17, Robby Valentine will perform in Spain for the first time since 2010. Together with his full band, he will play a unique double show at REVI LIVE, Madrid.

The night kicks off with a 90-minute set featuring his own work, including songs from his acclaimed album Embrace the Unknown, alongside classics like Over and Over Again, The Magic Breeze, and Love Takes Me Higher. He will then continue with Valentine’s Tribute to Queen, delivering the biggest hits and hidden gems such as Flick of the Wrist, Death on Two Legs, and Mustapha—all performed with the signature precision and attention to detail that he and his band are known for.
