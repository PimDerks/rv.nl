We used to love the silence  

We built a wall around us  

To play our solitary game  

The world of sacred magic  

Now looks like melted plastic  

All our pride turned into shame

The star that guides us  

Has surely lost it's way  

It makes us fight about  

Which words we want to say

Now there's only one thing  

For us to do  

Saying I love you  

Together we can make it through
