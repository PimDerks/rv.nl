<p>On March 6th 2024, <a href="https://kingrecords.net/">King Records</a> will put out a very special edition of the Embrace The Unknown album in Japan. As most of you will know, Japanese releases usually contain a few bonustracks to entice Japanese buyers to buy the Japanese editions of the albums instead of importing the regular versions.</p>

<p>The special edition of Embrace The Unknown includes three bonustracks: Radio Police, Running Deep Running High and a special version of Break The Chain entitled Chain Break Concerto. But that is not all! An entire second disc has been added featuring piano versions of <em>all</em> the tracks on the regular album!</p>
